## Second comment — method

The film is not a slideshow standing in for movement. It treats still images as choreographic units and the edit as a temporal score: cuts, holds, repetitions, accumulations, and transitions determine how the body is perceived.

The public project extends that proposition beyond one finite film. The same photographic corpus becomes an inspectable archive, an executable score, and a browser-based instrument.

## Alternate post B — process-first

A photographic archive does not have to remain evidence of a past performance. It can become a new performance system.

For **The Thing Without a Name**, I worked from one dancer session and treated each image as a choreographic unit. The body moves through cuts, holds, repetitions, opacity, scale, and duration rather than through continuous footage.

That finite 4:21 film now opens into **Danse Macabre**: film / score / archive / instrument.
