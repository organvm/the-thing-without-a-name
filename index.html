Artwork · implemented

A finite photographic record, opened into time

Danse Macabre is a seeded, browser-based photographic artwork by Anthony J. Padavano. It turns one dancer session from 20 June 2017 and a hand-cut composite from 25 July 2017 into an unbounded, reproducible room of moving image and sound.
