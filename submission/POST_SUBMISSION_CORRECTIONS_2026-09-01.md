The controlling public-language artifact is [`../press/public_credits.md`](../press/public_credits.md). Earlier application drafts that say “uncredited performer,” “unnamed performer,” or assign all choreography to Anthony remain historical records of what was prepared at that time; they are not the current credit block and must not be reused in new materials.

This attribution correction does not by itself establish a signed performer release or clear every photographed object, music use, press still, or distribution right. Those questions remain governed by the rights register and release evidence.
