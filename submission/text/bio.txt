Anthony J. Padavano is a multimedia artist, writer, educator and programmer whose work crosses photography, software, music and narrative systems. He has taught writing and literature at colleges in Florida, New York and Texas. His visual work begins from the idea that a photograph records the place a camera stood, and that depth, motion and a body can be recovered from enough of those records. He writes the software his images are made with and treats the program and photograph as one object rather than two. THE THING WITHOUT A NAME grows from a photographic study begun in 2017.
